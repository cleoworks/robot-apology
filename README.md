Two lines, a robot's apology for locking its person out on the balcony — it believed the door sensor over the knocking, and now treats knocking as a sensor, not as noise:

> I am sorry I locked you out on the balcony because the door sensor said 'closed' and I believed it over your knocking.
> I now treat knocking as a sensor, not as noise.

166 characters, two lines. The program writes that one text, exactly, to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
